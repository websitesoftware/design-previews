04How we workSix rules, nineteen years

Small enough toknow your code.

Build cost is paid once. Ownership cost is paid every year after, so we make decisions for the years after launch day.

  1. 01

    We write the code we ship. 

    Fewer third-party packages means fewer surprises when someone else pushes an update. You get software we fully understand.

  2. 02

    Two engineers read every line. 

    Nothing merges until a second developer has reviewed it. It costs us a little time and saves you a lot of it.

  3. 03

    The engineer you brief builds it. 

    No hand-offs between sales, design and delivery. The people in the first meeting are the people writing the code.

  4. 04

    You own everything. 

    Clean architecture, documentation and a handover any competent team could pick up. No lock-in, ever.

  5. 05

    Your day, plus a second shift. 

    Senior engineers on UK hours and a delivery team in India, so work keeps moving overnight.

  6. 06

    AI advises. People decide. 

    We use AI where it measurably helps, with review steps built in. A person makes every call that matters.

06Questions

Straight answers.

The questions we hear most. If yours isn't here, ask. You'll get a direct reply from an engineer.

  • 01Do we actually need a new website?

    Often, no. Restructuring your current CMS, connecting it to your other systems or adding AI to what you already have is frequently quicker and cheaper. We'll tell you which it is.

  • 02Can you take over an existing Sitecore or Umbraco site?

    Yes. We upgrade, restructure and support existing Sitecore, XM Cloud and Umbraco platforms, and extend them with .NET where needed. We start with an audit so there are no surprises.

  • 03How do you add AI without risking our data?

    Through a secure .NET layer with access controls. Only the data that's needed leaves your systems, answers are grounded in approved content, and people review output before it matters.

  • 04How long does a typical project take?

    Discovery takes one to two weeks and you'll click through a prototype within a month. From there we release working software every week until launch.

  • 05What happens after launch?

    The same team stays on for monitoring, patches and improvements. You can also take the code in-house at any time. It's documented and it's yours.
