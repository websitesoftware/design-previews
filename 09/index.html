Questions

Good questions we hear a lot.

If yours isn't here, ask us. We'll give you a straight answer.

Do we need a new website?

Often not. Restructuring your current CMS, connecting it to your other systems or adding AI to what you already have is frequently the smarter move.

Can you work with our existing Sitecore or Umbraco site?

Yes. We upgrade, restructure and support existing Sitecore, XM Cloud and Umbraco platforms, and extend them with .NET where needed.

How do you add AI safely?

Through a secure .NET layer with access controls. We avoid sending unnecessary or sensitive data to AI services, and people review AI output before it matters.

What does a CMS migration involve?

Content mapped and restructured for the new platform, a front end rebuilt for it, redirects and SEO protected, and clear goals agreed first. We plan it as a project, not a file transfer.

Why do you write your own code?

Fewer security risks, nothing breaks on someone else's update, and software you can own for years. Nothing ships until another developer has read it.
