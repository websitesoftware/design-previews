Questions people ask

Which content management systems do you work with?

Umbraco, Sitecore (including XM Cloud) and Optimizely, plus headless front ends built with Next.js.

Can you take over an existing Umbraco or Sitecore site?

Yes. We start by reviewing the code, hosting and editor workflows, fix what is risky first, and then improve the site in small, tested releases.

How do you protect search rankings during a migration?

Every old URL is mapped and redirected, titles, structured data and internal links are carried across, and pages are served as complete HTML so search engines and AI crawlers can read them without running JavaScript.
