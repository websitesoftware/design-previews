How we work

We put none in between.

The engineer who builds your software is the person you talk to, embedded in your team from the first discovery call to go-live. Decisions are made in the room, and working software arrives every week.

The layers most suppliers put in the way

  • Account manager

    Hears your problem. Writes it down.

  • Project office

    Turns the notes into tickets.

  • Delivery lead

    Hands the tickets to someone you have never met.

  • Hand-off

    Something comes back. It is not quite what you said.

Define. Design. Deliver.

  • Define

    A short discovery, together. We agree the problem worth solving and the question worth testing before anyone writes a line of code.

  • Design

    Architecture, interface and the constraints that matter, designed properly. A clickable prototype beats a forty-page proposal.

  • Deliver

    Working software in weeks, shipped at every step. Clean code, documented deployments, and a handover any competent team could pick up.

Questions people ask

Do you have account managers?

No. You deal directly with the engineers building your software, so decisions are made in the room and nothing is lost in translation.

What does a free proof of concept include?

We agree a focused problem and a question worth testing, then build something you can use and evaluate before agreeing a full project.

Who owns the code?

You do. We hand over clean code, documented deployments and a set-up any competent team could take on.
