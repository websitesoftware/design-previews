Questions people ask

What is a forward-deployed engineer?

An engineer who works inside your team rather than behind a supplier's account manager. The person who writes your software is the person you talk to, from the first discovery call to go-live.

Where is WebsiteSoft based?

WebsiteSoft (Website Software Ltd) is based in Bristol, UK, and has worked since 2007. Engineering partners in India extend the team when a project needs more hands.

How do projects start?

Usually with a short discovery and a free proof of concept whose scope we agree together, so you see working software before committing to a full project.
